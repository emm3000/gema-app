# Achievement Levels are decided by the teacher, never computed

The CNEB literal scale (AD, A, B, C) is qualitative. The Period Level for a
competency is a pedagogical judgment, so the app records Evidence Levels per
Activity and shows them to the teacher, but the Period Level is always entered
by the teacher. No averaging, mode, "last value" or any other rule exists in
the domain, and none may be added as a shortcut.
