# Play Console — Target audience and content (`docs/play/target-audience.md`)

Source for **App content > Target audience and content** in Play Console.

## Target age group

**Answer: 18 and over (adults only).**

Justification: Gema's only user is the teacher who owns the device. The
app's content (attendance registers, CNEB competency tracking, SIAGIE
export) is administrative software for a professional, not content
consumed or operated by children. Student names entered by the teacher are
classroom records, not a child-facing feature of the app.

## Is the app designed for children, or does it appeal to children?

**Answer: No, the app is not designed for children.**

Justification: There is no gameplay, no child-directed UI, no cartoon
characters, and no content a child would be expected to interact with
directly. The interface is built around school-administration vocabulary
(school years, periods, sections, achievement levels) aimed at a teacher's
workflow.

## Appeal to children under the Play Console's mixed-audience test

**Answer: No — the app does not appeal to children.**

Justification: Nothing in the visual style, mechanics, or marketing targets
a child audience; the app is a professional productivity tool distributed
under the Education category because of its subject matter (school
records), not because it is meant to be used by students.

## Ads

**Answer: The app contains no ads.**

Justification: Gema has no advertising SDK and shows no ads of any kind, so
none of the ads-related target-audience follow-up questions apply.

## COPPA / Play Families Policy applicability

**Answer: Not applicable — Gema is not directed at children and does not
opt into the Play Families Policy.**

Justification: consistent with the "18 and over" target audience answer and
with `docs/play/data-safety.md`, which confirms no data is collected from
anyone, child or adult.
